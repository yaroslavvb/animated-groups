Motion and optimizer settings
Density

The optimizer is preparing a start.

Exact every frame

Only the independent balls are stored. Every rotation, reflection, glide and lattice translate is applied again while drawing each frame.

Brownian, not scripted

A damped random force continuously perturbs velocity. The motion is generated live and does not repeat; the seed in the address reproduces its optimized starting state.

Symmetric impacts

Collisions are solved against nearby group images. The equal and opposite impulse is carried back through the same isometry, preserving both the bounce and the wallpaper symmetry.
