Finite translation orbits

Binary colorings of the 3 × 3 torus

All 32 patterns in {A, B}(ℤ/3ℤ)², modulo the nine translations and the global swap A ↔ B.

29 + 8 · 23 9 · 2 = 32

Canonical representative

First make B the minority color, then take the lexicographically least row-major word among all translations.

Drawing

Each 3 × 3 representative is repeated three times in each direction. Heavy lines mark fundamental domains; the central one is outlined.

Invariants

“Orbit” is the number of labeled patterns in the class. “Stabilizer” is its stabilizer order inside the 18-element group.

Color A Color B Color names are interchangeable.
