Space and time, coupled

Each row shows one spacetime symmetry with three different motifs. A generator acts on position and animation phase together: \((x,t)\mapsto(g\mathbin{\cdot}x,\pm t+\delta)\). The combined move is a symmetry even when its spatial and temporal parts are not symmetries separately.

In the candidate notation below, the spatial prefix uses standard Conway orbifold or rosette notation; the double brackets are proposed here. \(\tau_a\) advances normalized phase by \(a\), while \(\iota_b\) reverses it and then shifts by \(b\). Notation and limitations.

Every file has 60 frames at 20 fps, omits the duplicate endpoint, and sets loop=0 for infinite playback. The bounded catalog contains 468 normalized non-product actions over phase orders 2–12.

Paper connection

Relation to Ke and Wu

In Chenhang Ke and Congjun Wu's paper Two-Dimensional Space-Time Groups: Classification and Applications (arXiv:2604.05619), a general spacetime-group action is written as \((\mathbf r,t)\mapsto(R\mathbf r+\mathbf u,\,st+\tau)\) in Eq. (1). Rows 01, 02, 04, and 05 below visualize operations stated explicitly in the paper; row 06 uses its listed \(4'\) class. The remaining rows derive or specialize the same screw, glide, reversal, and mixed-translation mechanisms.

The paper does not use our candidate orbifold notation. The Cn/Dn titles here name displayed quotient or relay orders, not official identifiers in the paper's 275-group catalog. Our “graph-nonproduct” construction is not synonymous with the paper's “nonsymmorphic” classification, and \(\iota_b\) denotes playback reversal in these visuals—not antiunitary quantum time reversal.
