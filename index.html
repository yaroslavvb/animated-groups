The construction

A spatial move advances the clock.

A generator acts on a point and on animation phase at once: (x, t) ↦ (g·x, ±t + δ). The coupled operation is a symmetry even when its spatial and temporal factors are not symmetries separately.

Every GIF samples tk = k/N, omits the duplicate endpoint, and carries the infinite-loop extension. The saved files are decoded again to audit timing and the last-to-first seam.
